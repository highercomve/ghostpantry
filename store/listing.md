# GhostPantry

Short description: Track groceries, scan shelf photos and plan your pantry shopping.

Full description:
GhostPantry helps you organize the food in your pantry, fridge and freezer.

• Scan a shelf photo and review suggested foods.
• Select or correct each product, then review quantities and fill levels before saving.
• Group repeated products into one inventory entry.
• Track what you have and build a shopping list.
• Remember confirmed crop examples locally for similar future photos.

Choose the scanning method that suits your device: fast on-device scanning, supported system AI, a downloaded local language model on compatible devices, or a server you configure. Fast and local scanning require model downloads. Server scanning sends the selected photo to your chosen provider.

Suggestions can miss or misidentify foods, especially hidden products. Review names and quantities before adding them to inventory.

No GhostPantry account, advertising or analytics. Your inventory is stored on your device.
